# Inventory decision policy

- Reorder point is the store's minimum on-hand target for a product.
- Stock-out risk should be considered when estimated days of cover is at or below supplier lead time plus three days.
- Estimated weekly velocity is the latest month's units sold divided by four. This is a simple proxy, not a forecast model.
- Overstock means more than 20 units on hand and fewer than 3 units sold across the available sales period.
- Recommendations are decision support only; the manager should confirm supplier availability, promotions and local demand before placing an order.
